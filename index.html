關於我們 About Us

守登股份有限公司成立於 2026 年，總部位於台北，專營國際餐旅實習生媒合。我們引進來自印尼、泰國與菲律賓的餐旅科系學生，安排他們到台灣的飯店與餐旅業者實習，協助企業補充人力，也讓學生在國際環境中累積專業經驗。

Founded in 2026 and based in Taipei, SODÉN specializes in placing international hospitality interns with hotels and hospitality businesses across Taiwan. We bring hospitality students from Indonesia, Thailand, and the Philippines to train with Taiwanese employers, helping businesses meet staffing needs while giving students valuable professional experience.
